How to Login to Ancestry Account
================================

Ancestry is a genealogy platform that helps people explore family history, build family trees, and access historical records. This independent guide explains how to access an Ancestry account safely, troubleshoot common sign-in problems, and protect personal information.

What Is an Ancestry Account?
----------------------------

An Ancestry account provides access to your family tree, saved research, account settings, and any features included in your subscription. Some historical records and services may require a paid membership. The availability of features can vary by region and plan.

How to Login to Ancestry Account
--------------------------------

#. Open a browser and enter the official Ancestry website address directly: `www.ancestry.com <https://www.ancestry.com/>`_.
#. Select **Sign In** on the official website.
#. Enter the email address or username associated with your account, then provide your password when prompted.
#. If your account uses another supported sign-in method, choose the matching option shown on the official page.
#. Follow any identity-verification prompts and open your account dashboard.

Do not enter your password or verification code into a third-party support form. Check the website address before signing in.

How to Login on Windows or Mac
------------------------------

Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Visit the official Ancestry website, select **Sign In**, and enter your account credentials. If the page does not load correctly, update the browser or test a private window.

How to Login on Android or iPhone
---------------------------------

Install the official Ancestry app from your device's app store, confirm that the publisher is Ancestry, and open the app. Tap **Sign In** and use the same account credentials you use on the website. App screens may differ by version.

How to Reset a Forgotten Password
---------------------------------

#. Open the official Ancestry sign-in page.
#. Select the **Forgot Password** or equivalent recovery option.
#. Enter the email address associated with your account.
#. Follow the instructions sent by Ancestry to reset your password.
#. Return to the official sign-in page and try the new password.

If the recovery email does not arrive, check spam and junk folders and verify that you entered the correct address. Never share a password-reset link with anyone.

How to Fix Ancestry Login Problems
----------------------------------

Incorrect email or password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check for typing errors, unwanted spaces, and Caps Lock. Try the password-reset process if you cannot remember your password.

Login page not loading
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check your internet connection, update your browser, and temporarily disable extensions that interfere with sign-in. You can also try a different browser or device.

Verification or account access issues
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Use the verification method offered by Ancestry. If you cannot access the email address associated with the account, use the official account-recovery or help options. Do not send identity documents or security codes to an unverified contact.

App sign-in not working
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Update the Ancestry app, restart your device, and confirm that your internet connection is stable. Reinstalling the app may help if other steps fail; make sure you know your account credentials first.

How to Keep Your Account Secure
-------------------------------

* Use a strong, unique password.
* Turn on any additional account security features available to you.
* Sign out when using a shared device.
* Review unexpected account emails carefully and navigate to Ancestry directly rather than through unfamiliar links.
* Never share one-time codes, passwords, or recovery links with anyone claiming to provide support.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

Can I access my Ancestry account without a paid subscription?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

An account may still provide access to some saved information and free features, but paid records and tools can require an active subscription.

Can I use the same account on my phone and computer?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Yes. Use the same Ancestry account credentials on supported devices.

What should I do if I forgot my password?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Use the password-recovery option on the official Ancestry sign-in page.

Why does my password keep getting rejected?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check your credentials and sign-in method, and reset your password if necessary. If the issue persists, consult Ancestry's official help resources.

Where can I get official assistance?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Visit the official `Ancestry Support <https://support.ancestry.com/>`_ website for current account-help options.

Conclusion
----------

Most Ancestry login problems can be resolved by verifying your credentials, using the official recovery flow, or updating your browser or app. Always use official Ancestry pages for account access and security-sensitive tasks.
